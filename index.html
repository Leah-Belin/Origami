Fold Engine — 3D

Waterbomb base, in real 3D

Precrease folds still rotate a flap around a hinge — same idea as before. But the final collapse needs all 4 corners to converge on one point while the center stays put, which means the panels have to leave the flat plane. That's genuine 3D, rendered with Three.js instead of flat CSS transforms.

Steps 1–8 are the precrease: fold, then unfold, four times. Step 9 is the collapse — the 4 creases move together in one coordinated motion, not a chain of separate hinge folds, landing on the one 3D pose that keeps every panel's original side lengths intact. Front/back color compares each panel's current normal to its own flat-state normal — a fixed reference, not the camera — so it can't drift out of sync with the shape or change just because you rotated the view.
